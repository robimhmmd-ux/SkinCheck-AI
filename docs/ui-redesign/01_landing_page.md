## 📋 Prompt UI/UX Keyframe

```text
As an Expert UI/UX designer, create a keyframe based on DESIGN.md for the Public Landing Page of SkinCheck AI (home.html). 

Visual Identity & Aesthetic:
- Pure Bright Minimalist, clinical luxury digital wellness theme.
- Canvas background: Soft pearl slate (#f8fafc), Surface cards: Pure white (#ffffff) with crisp 1px hairline border (#e2e8f0) and subtle ambient shadow (rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)).
- Primary Brand Blue (#2563eb), Medical Derm Teal (#0d9488), and Deep Slate Typography (#0f172a).
- Font pairings: 'Outfit' for modern bold headings, 'Plus Jakarta Sans' for clear, human-centered body text.

Layout & Structural Sections:
1. Navigation Header:
   - Sticky blur navbar (backdrop-filter: blur(12px), background: rgba(255,255,255,0.85), border-bottom: 1px solid #e2e8f0).
   - Brand logo: Minimalist clinic emblem with 'SkinCheck' logotype and small blue AI badge.
   - Nav links: Fitur Utama, Cara Kerja, Pemerataan Sunscreen, Edukasi Kulit.
   - Right action buttons: Soft button 'Masuk' (#f1f5f9) and Primary CTA 'Mulai Scan Gratis' (#2563eb, rounded-sm: 8px).
2. Hero Section:
   - Headline (Outfit 700, 48px, #0f172a): "Analisis Kulit Wajah & Cek Pemerataan Sunscreen Berbasis AI Cerdas".
   - Subheadline (Plus Jakarta Sans, 18px, #64748b): "Deteksi kondisi kelembapan, minyak, jerawat, serta evaluasi perlindungan tabir surya Anda secara presisi langsung dari kamera browser tanpa instalasi."
   - Dual CTA Buttons: Primary button 'Coba Scan Wajah Sekarang' with icon bi-camera-fill (#2563eb) and Secondary pill button 'Pelajari Teknologi Sunscreen' with icon bi-play-circle (#0d9488).
   - Trust Badges: "98.4% Akurasi Diagnostik", "Diverifikasi Dermatologis", "Data Terenkripsi XAMPP MySQL".
   - Hero Visual: Split clinical mockup showcasing a customer face with an elegant AR bounding mesh overlay on the left, and a vibrant UV Sunscreen Heatmap distribution card on the right.
3. Feature Grid (3 Highlight Cards):
   - Card 1 (Skin Diagnostics): Micro-icon bi-stars in soft blue pill, headline "Deteksi 5 Parameter Kulit" (Hidrasi, Sebum, Pori-pori, Kemerahan, Tekstur).
   - Card 2 (Sunscreen Coverage AI): Micro-icon bi-sun-fill in soft teal pill, headline "Evaluasi Perlindungan UV & Sunscreen Coverage".
   - Card 3 (Tailored Routine & Ingredients): Micro-icon bi-shield-check in soft purple pill, headline "Rekomendasi Bahan Aktif & Produk Terverifikasi".
4. Interactive Step-by-Step Flow:
   - 4-step progressive horizontal cards with connecting hairline dotted lines: 1. Ambil Foto Wajah -> 2. Verifikasi OTP -> 3. Analisis Gemini AI -> 4. Dapatkan Laporan Klinis.
5. Social Proof & Clinical Metric Banner:
   - Clean statistics ticker: "15,000+ Scan Berhasil", "500+ Produk Terdaftar", "0.8s Rata-rata Latensi AI".
6. Minimalist Footer:
   - Pure white with hairline divider (#e2e8f0), medical disclaimer note, and copyright.
```
